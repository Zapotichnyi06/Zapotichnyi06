## Manques

**My own social app for iOS and Android, built to help people stay close across distance.**

I created [Manques](https://manques.app/) around a simple idea: everyday moments make relationships feel closer. It brings sharing and conversation into one place:

- **Share your world** — photo and video posts with front and back cameras, music, captions, and privacy controls.
- **Keep the conversation going** — direct messages, voice messages, and quick notes.
- **Make memories together** — Daily Moments and Duo Shot experiences with friends.
